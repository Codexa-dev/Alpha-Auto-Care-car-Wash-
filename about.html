About Alpha Auto Care

Alpha Auto Care is a vehicle detailing business focused on cleaning, protecting and caring for the vehicles we work on.

Our philosophy

We believe a well-cared-for vehicle starts with attention to detail and a clean finish. That means using suitable vehicle-care methods, speaking plainly with our customers, and keeping a professional standard of work.

Every vehicle is different, so we do not treat every surface the same way.

Our approach

Understand the vehicle

We start with your vehicle and what you want to improve.

Choose suitable services

We recommend services that fit the vehicle's surfaces and condition.

Carry out the work carefully

We work through the vehicle with care and keep you informed.

Our values

Attention to detail

Small areas matter, and we treat them that way.

Cleanliness

A clean vehicle is the goal of every job we do.

Honest communication

We explain what a service can and cannot do.

Professional standard

We aim for careful, consistent work on every vehicle.
